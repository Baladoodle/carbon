carbon / mockups

A design studio for Infinite's progression, as an Electron app on Omarchy. Band names, Y ranges and combat numbers are read from Infinite's assets/. The item, accessory and feat ideas are placeholder content that shows the form, not design decisions. Each window scrolls sideways on narrow screens.

1 · The Column

Infinite's Y axis is the canvas. Bands come from strata_layers.ron, the scale is a symmetric log around Y 0, and heights nobody has authored yet are hatched as unwritten. The faint shape behind the lanes is the danger envelope (±0.22 at the surface, widening with |Y|). Pressure notes sit in the right margin at the height they're about. The selected cell (Low Sky × Accessories) is where space opens a spread.

2 · A spread

Spot: Hollows × Accessories. Move: Fill. Direction: one line, not a chat. The candidates are placed on a field whose axes you choose, so near-duplicates sit together. You triage with single keys, and every kill carries a reason that feeds Taste. One slot is always a wildcard that ignores your taste rules.

3 · Curves, with the Bench open

The numbers lab. Mitigation uses Infinite's own formula, taken = raw·k/(k+def) with k = 100 read from combat.ron; the dots are the armor sets that are in the game. Below it, the intended defense budget for each band is a curve you drag, and cards far off it become pressure notes. On the right, the Bench shows one card. Each field is marked as written by you or by Claude, and the card can't be marked decided until Why is filled in.

4 · Taste

Taste, written down. On the left, canon imported from Infinite's docs with links to the source lines. In the middle, your own rules, each switchable per spread. On the right, patterns carbon noticed in your keeps and kills, which you promote to a rule or dismiss. This is what makes week 5 spreads better than week 1.
